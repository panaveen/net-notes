Electromagnetic wave based communications have been utilized for many decades
- 1. Radio and Television
- 2. Voice conversation (Cellphones)
- 3. Militaries

Early wireless quipment was expensive proprietary and not interoperable between vendors as it used unique and non standard modulation methods.
- 1880 - Photophone - Used between buildings in Washington DC - Alexander Graham Bell - Light was used.
- 1901 - Guglielmo Marconi transmitted and received the first transatlantic wireless Morse code signal—the letter "S" (three dots: ···)
- 1927 - First telephone service between NY and London
- 1924 - Bell Labs had created a two way voice carrying radio wave.
- 1940 - Voice technology was widely used.

Industry Organizations
- Regulations - FCC
- Standardization - IEEE
- Comptability - WiFi Alliance
